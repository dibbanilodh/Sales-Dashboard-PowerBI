# E-Commerce Sales Dashboard

An interactive **E-Commerce Sales Dashboard** built to analyze transaction data, tracking key performance indicators (KPIs) and profitability metrics across various categories, locations, and timeframes. 

---

## 📊 Key Insights & Metrics

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
*   **Total Revenue:** Overall income generated from sales transactions.
*   **Total Orders:** The cumulative count of successful purchases placed by customers.
*   **Total Profit:** Net financial gain after accounting for production and operational costs.
*   **Average Order Value (AOV):** The average amount per order.

### Sales & Profitability Breakdown
*   **Payment Preferences:** Tracks user behavior across payment modes including **Cash on Delivery (COD)**, **Credit Card**, **EMI**, **UPI**, and **Debit Card**.
*   **Geographic Performance:** 
    *   **Top States by Profit:** Visualizes regional profitability distribution, highlighting top-performing states such as **Madhya Pradesh** and **Maharashtra**.
    *   **Top Cities by Profit:** Breaks down revenue success down to the city level, identifying key hubs like **Indore** and **Pune**.
*   **Product Performance:**
    *   **Top 5 Categories by Profit:** Identifies the highest profit-driving inventory segments, including **Printers**, **Bookcases**, and **Sarees**.
    *   **Quantity Sold by Category:** Measures inventory movement volume across primary sectors: **Clothing**, **Electronics**, and **Furniture**.
*   **Monthly Profit Trends:** Tracks seasonal performance fluctuations over a **12-month calendar cycle** to call out peak promotional periods and structural dips.

---

## 📌 Features
*   **Dynamic Visualizations:** Includes donut charts, area graphs, bar charts, line trends, and interactive progress rings.
*   **Interactive Filters:** Fully customizable filtering options sorted by quarters (**Qtr 1, Qtr 2, Qtr 3, Qtr 4**) and state to drill down into specific subsets of data.
*   **Trend Tracking:** Monthly bar charts highlight positive versus negative profit margins instantly using color-coded variables.
